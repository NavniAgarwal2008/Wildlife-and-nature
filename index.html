Our Conservation Projects

<

🌳 Reforestation

Reforestation restores forests by planting trees in deforested areas. It helps fight climate change, protect wildlife, and support communities.

Impact: Thousands of trees planted, cleaner air, and revived habitats.

🌊 Ocean Cleanup

Ocean Cleanup focuses on removing plastic waste and pollutants from oceans and rivers. Millions of tons of plastic enter the seas every year, harming marine life and ecosystems. This project aims to clean existing pollution and prevent new waste from reaching the ocean.

🐾 Wildlife Rescue

Wildlife Rescue focuses on saving injured, orphaned, or displaced animals and rehabilitating them back into their natural habitats. It’s about giving vulnerable creatures a second chance at life.
